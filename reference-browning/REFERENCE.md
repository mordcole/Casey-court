# Reference photographs — James R. Browning U.S. Court of Appeals Building

The visual reference for `courtroom.html` is a set of five public-domain
photographs (Carol M. Highsmith Archive, Library of Congress; surfaced via
PICRYL) of the James R. Browning U.S. Court of Appeals Building, San
Francisco (1905, Beaux-Arts).

The binary JPEGs could not be committed from the build environment: its
network policy blocks all image hosts (picryl.com, loc.gov, wikimedia —
403 at the egress proxy), so the photographs were supplied by the project
owner in-chat and viewed there. This file records the sources and what
each shows, and stands in for the binaries.

## What was viewed (five photos supplied in-session)

- **Courtroom One — THE HERO (two near-identical frames).** Cove/groin-vaulted
  cream plaster ceiling densely crusted with cast ornament (winged figures in
  the vault corners), three recessed glowing laylight coffers ringed by
  marquee-style strings of bare bulbs, full-height book-matched Pavonazzo
  marble with dramatic grey-violet veining, engaged columns and pilasters
  with carved capitals, attic storey with caryatid herms flanking a small
  allegorical mosaic lunette, and behind the long dark-mahogany en-banc
  bench (~11 black leather chairs, centre chair tallest) a red-breccia
  marble aedicule framing a large gold-ground diamond-lozenge geometric
  mosaic; brass bar rail on marble piers, grey-blue carpet, white mosaic
  tile band at the bar, red marble curbs.
- **Second courtroom (Siena room).** Flat coffered ceiling with downlights,
  golden Yellow Siena pilasters and wainscot framing Carrara panels on
  verde-antique bases, white caryatid statues flanking the 3-seat bench,
  pierced gilt-bronze filigree railings, grey carpet.
- **Third courtroom.** Coffered ceiling with central laylight, brass stem
  chandeliers with white glass globes, blue-grey veined marble, pedimented
  door surrounds, arched fanlight windows, 3-seat bench, globe torchieres.
- **Mosaic lunette close-up (drives the tesserae shader).** Enthroned female
  figure in violet-blue robe with ermine-trimmed stole holding a glass orb,
  eagle wings behind, nude child with laurel, two kneeling attendants
  (russet robe; blue coat with red cloak), gold-to-orange sunset sky over a
  dark blue-green tree line; individual tesserae with mortar joints.
